The problem

Patient demand is growing. Clinical operations are the bottleneck.

Most health companies can sell the consultation, prescribe the plan or order the test. The hard part is getting samples collected reliably, at scale, with a patient experience that protects the brand.

Nurse recruitment is slow and ongoing
Scheduling across patients and staff is messy
Patient no-shows create friction and cost
Regional and outer-metro coverage is hard
Collection quality directly affects results
Internal teams get pulled into logistics

How it works

A simple, reliable flow

  1. 1

    Referral received

    Partner sends a referral or booking request.

  2. 2

    We coordinate

    Express arranges the patient and the collection.

  3. 3

    Collection completed

    A qualified nurse completes the collection.

  4. 4

    Sample delivered

    Sample reaches your required pathology provider.

  5. 5

    Results returned

    Partner receives results through their normal pathway.

We don't need to own the patient relationship. Express can operate as a white-label or partner-facing clinical services layer — your brand stays front and centre throughout.

Service models

Three ways to collect

Mobile collections

Clinic collection pathway

Corporate / onsite collections

Nurses deployed onsite for workplace or group testing programs, run as a coordinated event.
