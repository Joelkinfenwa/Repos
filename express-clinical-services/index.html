How it works

A simple, reliable flow

  1. 1

    Referral received

    Partner sends a referral or booking request.

  2. 2

    We coordinate

    Express arranges the patient and the collection.

  3. 3

    Collection completed

    A qualified nurse completes the collection.

  4. 4

    Sample delivered

    Sample reaches your required pathology provider.

  5. 5

    Results returned

    Partner receives results through their normal pathway.
