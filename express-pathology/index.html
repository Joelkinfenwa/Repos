FAQ

Questions partners ask

Which areas do you actually cover?

Every state and territory — metro, regional and many remote locations. Send us your site list and we'll confirm coverage and SLAs for each postcode.

Can results flow into our existing systems?

Yes. We support API, HL7/LIS integration and a partner portal so status updates and results land in your PMS, LIS or case-management system.

How are your clinicians credentialed?

All clinicians hold current AHPRA registration where applicable, national police checks, immunisation records, professional indemnity and complete our competency and induction program.

What volumes can you handle?

Anything from a handful of ad-hoc visits to managed programs of thousands of collections per month, with rostering and reporting handled by your account team.

How quickly can we start?

After a short scoping call we can typically onboard standard programs within days. Complex, multi-site contracts follow an agreed implementation plan.
